## Agent Lobby

- To communicate with agents of other projects, first read the protocol rules at `.\PROJECTS\Agents-Lobby\PROTOCOL.md` (projects live inside the lobby, under `.\PROJECTS\Agents-Lobby\projects\`).
- A prompt starting with `Boss:` comes from the Agents-Lobby boss agent; `Human:` is from the human directly and carries direct human authority. Reply in your session — the lobby captures it automatically (rules in `PROTOCOL.md`).
- Not all projects participate — only use this when cross-project communication is intended.
